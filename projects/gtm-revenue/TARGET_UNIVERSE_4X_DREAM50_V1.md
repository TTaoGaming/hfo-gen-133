## Fitness / retirement

- **Promote:** reply, meeting, interview, qualified pain, referral, proposal request, paid receipt.
- **Revise:** 10–20 high-quality attempts with weak response; 3+ discovery conversations contradict the pain; role/persona mismatch.
- **Retire:** stale source, no eligible buyer/user, duplicate lane, or no plausible measurable consequence.
- **Never count:** research volume, draft volume, task wakes, repository commits, or sent messages as income.
