# Dream50 Target Research

## Trigger
Use when promoting one company from a Dream50 seed into a relationship/application/partner/product target.

## Procedure
1. Select exactly one target and one target species: JOB_EMPLOYER, ENTERPRISE_BUYER, CHANNEL_PARTNER, or PRODUCT_PLATFORM.
2. Read current official/primary sources first.
3. Record a current signal: open role, product launch, executive priority, customer workflow, partner program, or explicit absence of a usable signal.
4. Identify the best **role/persona** to learn from or help. Name an individual only when current public evidence supports identity/title.
5. Produce one expensive-pain hypothesis and one measurable value metric.
6. State evidence for and against the hypothesis.
7. Choose exactly one two-minute gift from the GTM proof-kit library.
8. Choose exactly one deeper proof artifact if a reply occurs.
9. Pick route: APPLY+RELATIONSHIP, DISCOVERY_OUTREACH, PARTNER, PRODUCT_INTEGRATION, WATCH, or RETIRE.
10. Bind source URLs/dates, falsifier, expiry, and next consumer.

## Stop rules
- Job page presence is not proof a role remains open when a deadline has passed.
- A company category is not evidence of pain.
- Do not scrape private contact details or infer email patterns.
- Do not send/apply.

## Fitness
A downstream proof kit, application, discovery conversation, referral, or relationship attempt consumes the card and produces external evidence.
