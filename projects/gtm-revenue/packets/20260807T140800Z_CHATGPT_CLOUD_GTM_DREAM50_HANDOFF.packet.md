## 1. Why this packet exists

The prior system could build sophisticated artifacts but had no external income receipt. The operator's first SCORE mentoring session with David Eaton supplied a concrete commercial frame:

1. Discover **what businesses actually need**, with process-improvement / Six-Sigma instincts.
2. Quantify consequence using **headcount, margin, cycle time, risk, AI/model spend, error/rework, or revenue**.
3. Look for a large value delta; $10k fee for credible $100k value is a useful example, not a law.
4. Practice discovery with existing friends, especially senior people.
5. Build relationships with companies **whether or not they are hiring**.
6. Treat the job search as two linked systems: official applications + relationship creation.
7. Build a Dream-50 and a CRM.
8. Hyperpersonalize around **their probable need**, not around a catalog of technologies.
9. Iterate on interviews, replies, conversations, proposals, and income rather than internal confidence.

### B — Enterprise buyer
Goal: find one expensive workflow at each target and build a relationship before proposing implementation.

Primary vertical hypotheses:

- healthcare: administrative/revenue-cycle/claims/prior-auth operations;
- retail/logistics/industrial: high-volume exception/handoff/process work;
