# X13 Slack read-channel phase 4 decision: DEFER

The four-wake Slack `read_channel` campaign is closed without an additional Slack candidate call.

Two bounded reads succeeded and one synthetic invalid-channel probe failed closed. That is enough to retain the connector as a human-reviewed catalog seam, but not enough to adopt it operationally. The wrapper did not establish effective identity, scopes, membership, workspace, rate class, quota use, raw Slack parity, history completeness, retention, or immutability. Its concise output also exposed full message content, author email, and a cursor transiently.

Operational adoption is therefore deferred. Reopening requires a named operational consumer and acknowledgment, a bound work item, a distinct authorized raw Slack verifier for matched success and failure cases, identity and permission evidence, quota or rate evidence, and measured operator value. Adoption and fitness credit remain zero.
