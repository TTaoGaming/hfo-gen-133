# X13 Slack `read_channel` phase 3

One bounded, read-only, limit-one probe against a synthetic nonexistent channel identifier returned `channel_not_found`. No message content, retry, fallback, secondary read, or mutation occurred.

The connector failed closed for this probe. The error is not proof that a channel is absent: the wrapper itself states that the identifier may be invalid, belong to another workspace, or be inaccessible to the app. Slack documents `channel_not_found` for an invalid channel value and separately documents permission, membership, scope, rate-limit, and transient-service errors.

Operational consumers must fail closed, avoid retrying the unchanged identifier, and obtain separate evidence for workspace, identity, scope, membership, and channel existence before classifying absence. Adoption and fitness credit remain zero.
