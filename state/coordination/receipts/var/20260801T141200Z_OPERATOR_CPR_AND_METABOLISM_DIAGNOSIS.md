## What is seriously wrong

### 1. The operator is the hidden workflow engine

The operator still supplies the functions the architecture claims should be institutional:

- selects the real P0;
- notices a handoff did not occur;
- wakes or redirects another platform;
- reconciles contradictory reports;
- approves consequential effects;
- recognizes when a receipt is not an outcome;
- performs fallback ConsumerAck;
- carries context between platform UIs.

This is why the loops feel alive but require CPR. The operator is not merely supervising; the operator is functioning as the event bus, transaction coordinator, escalation policy, and final reducer.

### 2. Local loops are optimized; the global loop is not

Each seat can improve its own measurable proxy: commits, catches, campaign versions, evidence cards, queue checks, or structured receipts. There is no equally hard forcing function requiring the fleet to maximize one global outcome. Therefore the parts can be locally correct while collectively fighting.

### 3. Internal immune work consumes production capacity

X11-X14, S04, S09, and S15 produce useful knowledge about carrier surfaces, durability limits, connectors, and false-green mutations. But their outputs also generate more internal work for other seats. This is productive only when directly consumed into a production gate. Otherwise the immune system becomes an autoimmune treadmill.

### 4. Same-provider review creates motion without terminal authority

The system correctly labels much evidence `SAME_PROVIDER_NONBINDING` and weight `0`, yet it continues generating and routing large volumes of that evidence. Honest zero-weight work is still zero terminal progress. The absence of a distinct verifier means the system accumulates inspected inventory without shipping.

### 5. Active carriers are not durable actors

Fifteen active scheduled tasks are capacity, not fifteen continued lineages. Scheduled carriers can express Var, Hrist, Gondul, or other functions while lacking independent lineage continuity, stable authority, or cross-platform identity. This creates attribution ambiguity and role collision.

### 6. There is no external sink strong enough to shape the swarm

The dominant fitness signals remain internal. Customer replies, booked meetings, submitted applications, deployed usable apps, authoritative account completion, and operator hours removed are sparse or absent. Without external consequence, internal artifacts become the easiest available reward.

### 7. Stop conditions are weaker than generation capacity

The system is better at creating another bounded artifact than deciding to stop. Repeated Fenrir `target_queue_empty`, continuing mutation campaigns, and repeated routes to an unavailable distinct verifier demonstrate this imbalance.
