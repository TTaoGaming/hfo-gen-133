## Vote

`REVISE` — select `REVISE_PUBLIC_ONLY_TECHNICAL_FIT_GATE_FIRST`.

NSF 26-510 is real and current, but the admission card does not yet justify pulling private eligibility data or spending up to an hour on a broad fit matrix. The decisive uncertainty is earlier and cheaper: whether HFO can be compressed into one NSF-grade high-risk technical innovation with a measurable R&D uncertainty, a credible baseline against existing solutions, and non-invented demand. If that cannot be done from public repository evidence, company eligibility is irrelevant and operator time should remain untouched.

The stage-0 gate must not draft Project Pitch prose. Its purpose is to kill or narrow the lane cheaply. Passing it authorizes only a later request for a private eligibility check; it does not authorize an account, application, pitch, proposal, outreach, or submission.

### ACCEPT_FULL_NO_SEND_FIT_MATRIX

Evidence for:

- NSF 26-510 is active, Phase I can fund up to $305,000, and the Project Pitch is intentionally smaller than a full proposal.
- The card already identifies plausible technical-risk, spatial-access, resource-access, and evaluation hypotheses.
- A no-send matrix is reversible and can expose eligibility or fit blockers before application work.
- The November 4 deadline creates a real timing cost to indefinite delay because official Project Pitch feedback commonly takes one to two months.

Evidence against:

- Four possible themes are evidence of unresolved scope, not a pitch-ready innovation.
- The current repository state does not establish novelty, prior-art separation, customer demand, or a single high-risk R&D question.
- The golden app lacks distinct verification and browser/customer evidence; workflow safety artifacts are mostly same-provider internal evidence.
- The full matrix can consume 35-60 consumer minutes and trigger 20-40 operator minutes before the cheaper technical-fit question is answered.
- The standing income lane remains at $0; a federal grant path has low near-term cash value and can displace faster job, client, or product work.

Conclusion: admissible only after the public stage-0 gate passes.

### REVISE_PUBLIC_ONLY_TECHNICAL_FIT_GATE_FIRST

Evidence for:

- It attacks the highest-value uncertainty without credentials, private data, or operator relay.
- NSF explicitly requires a core high-risk technical innovation, concrete R&D challenges, market opportunity, and team suitability; feature lists and routine engineering are insufficient.
- A 20-minute cap limits grant-chasing and architecture theater.
- A hard `MISSING_DEMAND_EVIDENCE` or `ROUTINE_INTEGRATION` result gives a useful stop receipt.
- The experiment is reversible and leaves every world-effect gate closed.

Conclusion: best expected value only if strictly time-boxed and treated as a kill gate, not a pitch draft.
