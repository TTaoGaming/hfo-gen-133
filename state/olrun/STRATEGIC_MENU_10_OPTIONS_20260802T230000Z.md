## ARCHETYPE A — SERVICES (sell your labor as engineering hours)

You do the work. Client pays per project. Fast cash, no product IP compounding.

### 1. AI Integration Rescue — cold email to broken-AI startups
- **30d cash P:** 12-20%
- **6-12mo ceiling:** $5-15k/mo billings + $249-750/mo × N managed retainers
- **Pitch:** "I turn broken AI prototypes and manual workflows into tested, deployed systems." Price ladder: $350 diagnostic → $1,500-3,000 sprint → $249-750/mo managed.
- **Swarm handles:** draft 25 personalized emails/day, verify pre-send, log receipts (H autonomy after class-approve)
- **You handle:** sign the envelope once, respond to interested replies (calls only for close prospects), deliver the actual work
- **First move:** bind real Cal.com link, sign `ENV_COLD_EMAIL_001`, verify Instantly warmed domain
- **Infrastructure ready?** ⭐ Yes — warmed domain `tryagentreleasegate.com` sitting unused

### 2. Upwork targeted proposals — bid on posted AI/dev jobs
- **30d cash P:** 25-45% (highest raw cash P in the menu)
- **6-12mo ceiling:** $5-15k/mo + reputation compounds
- **Pitch:** same AI Integration Rescue offer, applied to specific job posts
- **Swarm handles:** monitor Upwork feed, draft proposal candidates, match past work
- **You handle:** ⚠️ **per-proposal hand-writing** — Upwork ToS forbids bulk automation; this is instance-babysitting
- **First move:** verify/create Upwork account, write first 5 proposals manually to calibrate
- **Trade-off:** highest raw P, highest operator time cost — only pick if cash > attention this month

### 3. Agency subcontract — become a hidden hand behind consultancies
- **30d cash P:** 15-35% for first engagement
- **6-12mo ceiling:** $3-10k/mo steady from 2-3 agency relationships
- **Pitch:** "White-label AI integration work for your agency. Fixed scope, evidence-first delivery."
- **Swarm handles:** identify agencies via public directories, draft outreach, track responses
- **You handle:** 2 partner contacts/day (email-based mostly), 1 intro call per week
- **First move:** list 20 small AI/dev agencies from Clutch or LinkedIn, draft outreach template
- **Trade-off:** borrowed trust + deal flow, but you're a subcontractor not a brand

## ARCHETYPE B — PRODUCTS (build once, sell copies)

You build a thing. Distribute cold. Revenue compounds if it lands. Slower to first dollar.

### 4. AI-dev tool ship — MCP server, agent monitor, receipt SDK
- **30d cash P:** 5-15%
- **6-12mo ceiling:** $5-30k/mo MRR if it lands + community moat
- **Pitch:** small tool for AI builders that solves ONE pain you personally hit in HFO. Sell $19-49/mo SaaS OR $29-99 one-time Gumroad.
- **Swarm handles:** research 5 candidate pains, build MVP in 2 weeks, deploy Cloudflare Pages, GitHub public repo, cold email to AI startups, draft Reddit posts
- **You handle:** pick the pain, review MVP before ship, post Reddit thread yourself (authenticity), reply to comments 48hr
- **First move:** commission swarm to research + spec 5 candidate ideas (1-page brief each)
- **Fit note:** you ARE the ICP — every pain you hit in 18mo of HFO, thousands of AI devs are feeling right now

### 5. Desktop utility ship — meeting transcription, prompt library, MCP debugger
- **30d cash P:** 5-10%
- **6-12mo ceiling:** $20-50k/mo proven (MacWhisper by Jordi Bruin)
- **Pitch:** small native/electron app, one-time purchase, Gumroad or Stripe direct, no gatekeeper
- **Swarm handles:** build the app (Electron + Cloudflare Workers backend), landing page, demo video, cold email
- **You handle:** pick the niche (dictation? prompts? MCP debug?), first customer support, Reddit post
- **First move:** pick niche, get feature list to swarm
- **Trade-off:** highest single-product ceiling in the menu, but slower 30d P

### 6. Micro-SaaS small unit — dev-adjacent tool, John Rush template
- **30d cash P:** 3-8%
- **6-12mo ceiling:** $600/mo per unit × N units — John Rush proved 24 units = $3M/yr combined
- **Pitch:** tiny B2B utility (e.g., AI-agent status page, prompt versioning directory, MCP registry). $9-29/mo SaaS.
- **Swarm handles:** build MVP in 1 week, deploy, SEO landing page, cold email to niche
- **You handle:** pick the niche, launch, first users
- **First move:** pick niche and micro-pain
- **Trade-off:** low per-unit revenue, compounds as portfolio if you ship 5+ over 6 months

## ARCHETYPE C — DISTRIBUTION-FIRST (attention, then monetize)

Build audience or attention capital. Convert later. High long-term value, low direct 30d cash.

### 7. Reddit AppAlchemy pattern — build tiny thing, post authentic
- **30d cash P:** 5-12% (if attached to a specific product; otherwise 0)
- **6-12mo ceiling:** $17k MRR proven (AppAlchemy) if it hits; usually $500-2k/mo
- **Pitch:** build small tool solving ONE narrow pain, post to r/LocalLLaMA or r/AIAgents leading with the pain, mention tool once at the end, reply to every comment for 48hr
- **Swarm handles:** research pain patterns from subreddit history, draft post variants, build the tool
- **You handle:** post yourself (authenticity is the product), reply to every comment for 48hr
- **First move:** pick subreddit you already read, scan last 30 days for pain patterns
- **Trade-off:** works cold, needs you as the human voice — cannot be automated

### 8. Daily audience build (LinkedIn / X / Substack, swarm-drafted)
- **30d cash P:** <5% direct
- **6-12mo ceiling:** 500-2k engaged followers → enables everything else in year 2
- **Pitch:** publish 1 post/day across 3 platforms — technical HFO learnings, receipt discipline, AI dev patterns. Swarm drafts, you approve/edit.
- **Swarm handles:** draft posts from HFO chain-rows, format per platform, schedule
- **You handle:** 5-min approve daily, occasional voice-edit
- **First move:** pick 1 platform, commit to 30-day post streak
- **Trade-off:** compound investment — no clean cold case exists for building audience from zero and monetizing inside 12mo

### 9. HN Show submission pipeline — one-shot amplification
- **30d cash P:** 3-15% (very high variance)
- **6-12mo ceiling:** one hit = 10k-100k visitors + press
- **Pitch:** every shipped tool gets one HN Show submission with a genuine title
- **Swarm handles:** monitor for shipped artifacts, draft HN Show post copy, wait for optimal submit window
- **You handle:** submit under your account, respond to top comments in first 2 hours
- **First move:** ship one thing worth submitting (see options 4/5/6)
- **Trade-off:** fastest fail signal in the menu (3-7 days), but ceiling is a lottery
