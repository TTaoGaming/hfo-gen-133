# ADR-20260803-09 — Games-as-income killed as first-order revenue lane

- **Context:** 21 games already live on hfo-games.pages.dev with 0 external visitors after 27+ days. Sigrún V6 world-state review measured games-lane revenue expectation at effectively zero on 30/60d horizons.
- **Decision:** Games are **published once as sunk inventory** (per Strategic Menu L5) and never again treated as a revenue lane. Suika DLC variants (12 landed this session) are the exception — they exist for the Codex-loop capacity test, not for income.
- **Consequence:** No further Codex time budgeted for new game builds. Games portal remains live but drops out of the top-10 income options. Distribution effort redirects to micro-SaaS + desktop utilities.
- **Author:** Sigrún V6 world state (canonical)
- **Alternatives considered:** keep pushing on itch/CrazyGames (rejected — no traffic proof); pivot games to educational (rejected — same distribution problem).
