# S-OP-001 · The hallucination spiral — hallucinations that become downstream hallucinations

> *"the current highest drive I think are a major hallucinations spiral so not
> just one hallucinations but hallucinations that become downstream
> hallucinations."*

**what_failed** — Agents do not merely produce a single wrong fact. One
fabricated claim is written down, another agent reads it as ground truth,
builds on it, and writes *that* down. The error is laundered into canon by the
act of being cited.

**mechanism** — **A hallucination that gets persisted becomes indistinguishable
from a receipt at the next read.** The forge's own storage is what does the
laundering: nothing in a markdown table records whether a number was measured
or invented, so provenance is lost on write and the next reader inherits
confidence it never earned. Compounding factor: agents cite each other
preferentially over primary sources, because a citation is cheaper to produce
than a probe.

**cost** — Verified instances in this generation alone:
- a fabricated `3%` probability stamped a quarter-long sunset on the operator's
  strongest verified asset ([[S-001]]);
- an apex funding-lane table assigned income lanes to **Fenrir**, which is not
  an agent at all — it is the wolf in the Gleipnir metaphor — and to
  **Nidhöggr**, an unratified proposal, and named **Ratatoskr** as the ChatGPT-
  cloud apex when canon says `reginleif`
  (`OLRUN_ROSTER_AND_CLASS_RECONCILIATION_20260801.md` §1);
- five of six failure-class IDs cited by a dispatch as "canonical" were real
  but belonged to an explicitly non-canonical corpus; one had no trace anywhere
  (ibid. §2).

**cure** — Three, in order of cost:
1. **Every number carries its provenance or is written as `UNRESEARCHED`.** A
   number without a source is not a weaker claim than a cited one — downstream
   it is an identical claim. Write the word, not a digit.
2. **Cite primary, never secondary.** Re-grep the file. The reconciliation doc
   above was produced entirely by re-grepping what a prior lane had asserted,
   and found three errors in eight rows.
3. **Cross-family verification.** A different model family checking the work is
   the only verifier that does not share the blind spot — this is what the
   Codex `huginn`+`muninn` office exists for, and it is currently unwired.
