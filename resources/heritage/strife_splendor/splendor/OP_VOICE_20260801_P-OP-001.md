# P-OP-001 · The $0 free mesh — near-frontier compute at no cost, proven

> *"one of the cool things of what we're doing is the $0 free mesh. I've
> already proven it somehow. I think in your system you think free is less
> capable, but that's not true in this sense. but it's an almost unlimited
> source of compute with near Frontier models. you just got to be able to
> harness it."*

**what_worked** — Eight vendor families reachable at zero marginal cost (groq,
cerebras, sambanova, cohere, mistral, gemini, openrouter ×2), carrying models
the operator characterizes as near-frontier. Operator-attested as proven.

**mechanism** — Free tiers are **customer-acquisition spend by the vendor, not
a quality tier.** The models behind a free tier are frequently the same weights
served to paying customers; what is throttled is rate and context, not
capability. Aggregating eight independent free tiers converts a per-vendor rate
limit into an aggregate throughput that no single vendor's limit constrains.

> **Operator correction to Sigrún, recorded because it is load-bearing:** *"in
> your system you think free is less capable, but that's not true in this
> sense."* This is a named prior in the substrate — free ⇒ inferior — and it is
> wrong here. Prior canon scored the $0-mesh lane at ~10% partly on this prior.
> **Any future lane reasoning about the mesh must read this row first.**

**evidence** — operator attestation, 2026-08-01. Eight vendor families named in
`SUBSTRATE_ROSTER.md` v0_2 §3.6.

**bar_not_met** — ⚠️ **This row does NOT meet the splendor bar in
`contracts/strife_splendor_rehydration.v0_1.md` §2**, which requires *verified
external effect a stranger could see or touch* plus *consumer acceptance*. The
mesh is internal capability. No stranger has seen its output; no consumer has
accepted anything from it. It is admitted at `PARTIAL` deliberately rather than
by softening the bar — the contract's §7 explicitly warns against that.

Worse for the row: the mesh is currently ⛔ **STUCK** (`surtr`, blocker B5), and
its diagnostic was written and never run. **A proven capability that does not
currently run is a claim about the past.**

**PARTIAL_ADMISSION: needs_operator_amplification_for_evidence** — a receipt of
the mesh serving a request (vendor, model, timestamp, output) would move this
from attestation to record, and would let the next lane reason about it without
re-litigating the "free is weak" prior.
