## §B · Base-rate exaggerations

- **B1 · IndieHackers median revenue.** Report D says "first revenue within 6 months, not 30 days" (§0) and quietly implies P(first $500 in 30 days) of 8–15% for the top channels. Actual: IndieHackers/TrustMRR data historically shows median solo indie first-year MRR is **$0**; ~60–70% never reach $500 MRR at any point. The 8–15% probability the report puts on a *cold-start's single Show HN* is 5–10x the empirical rate of *any* solo indie hitting $500 in month 1.
- **B2 · Show HN base rate.** Report claims 8–15% cold-P (§2.1). Median Show HN scores 2 points ([Show HN by the Numbers](https://danfking.github.io/blog/2026/04/23/show-hn-by-the-numbers/)); ~28,000 Show HN posts/year in 2025 with only a small fraction reaching front page. Comments do NOT predict stars (r=0.10 same source). Realistic front-page hit rate for a first-time cold submitter is **1–3%**, not 8–15%. Report is off by ~4x.
- **B3 · Cold email reply rate.** P says "3.43%" and "up to 18%" (base rate anchors). Both true — but for **warmed** domains with 30+ days ramp. For a $0 operator with no domain history, week-1 realistic is under 1% inbox after Gmail spam filter. The report's "3–8%" cold-P for Instantly-based cold email (D §3.1) collapses when you divide by (spam-hit % × unopened % × unreplied %).
- **B4 · Reddit account throttle.** Report D §2.2 flags "30+ day account age, 100+ karma" as the ceiling. Since late 2025 many big subs (r/LocalLLaMA, r/programming) tightened to 90+ day age and shadow-remove first posts from any account under 500 karma. The report's 5–10 hrs/wk karma-farming estimate is optimistic by 2x.
- **B5 · Newsletter sponsorship CTR × conversion.** Report P quotes 60–250 clicks per issue for engaged AI/dev lists (Base rates). For a $19–49/mo product, typical trial→paid conversion on a cold-audience click is 0.5–2%. So a $200 Ben's Bites unclassified slot yielding, say, 100 clicks → 0.5–2 paid signups → **$10–100 first-month revenue** on $200 spent. Report calls this "the single best small-budget entry point." It is a **losing unit-economics play at cold start**.
- **B6 · Passionfroot creator response rate.** Report P #1-ranks Passionfroot because "every creator listed has already declared they take sponsorships and priced them publicly" (§A17, §F1). What the report doesn't establish: response rate to bookings from **unknown $0-revenue solo devs**. Passionfroot's traction case studies are ElevenLabs, Figma, Replit — brands. Cold-start booking acceptance for a no-brand solo is `[UNVERIFIED]` and probably materially worse.

## §C · Missing failure modes

- **C1 · Reddit new-account throttle 2025–2026.** Neither report mentions that Reddit began systematic throttling of accounts under 90 days posting in "product/tool" contexts. Silent removal, not warnings. `[UNVERIFIED specific policy citation but widely reported in r/modhelp]`.
- **C2 · Warmed-domain drift.** Report D §3.1 acts as if warmup is one-time. Domain reputation drifts every 60–90 days without sustained volume; a solo who sends 500 emails then pauses re-hits spam on relaunch.
- **C3 · Directory acceptance rate.** Report D §2.13 lists 30 directories, all treated as auto-accept. In reality G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, Crunchbase require verified customer reviews / funding data — a $0-revenue no-customer solo will be **rejected outright** at those five, which are 5/30 of the "curated" list.
- **C4 · Agency subcontract non-compete lockout.** Report P §B recommends Toptal/A.Team/Arc as fast cash. Toptal's contractor agreement includes 12-month non-solicit on any client contact touched via the platform. If a Toptal client would have been a natural direct customer of your SaaS, you're contractually blocked. Not mentioned.
- **C5 · Newsletter sponsorship re-book rates.** Report P treats sponsorships as replicable ("upgrade tier for creators whose A/B test converted," §Weeks 9–12). Newsletter sponsors typically don't re-book underperforming indie sponsors even if attribution was OK — because the newsletter operator prioritizes ad-market advertisers who buy quarters. Solo indies get slotted into "one-and-done" inventory.
- **C6 · Show HN karma exhaustion.** You get one Show HN per project. Report D mentions this but doesn't flag: a botched first Show HN with cherry-picked launch time = your permanent record. No do-over on the same URL.
- **C7 · Rewardful / affiliate program cold-start = 0 affiliates.** Report P §F4 says "the operator's compounding lever." Affiliate programs at $0 MRR with no reputation attract **zero organic signups**. Report doesn't say you need to recruit affiliates one-by-one, cold, before the program does anything.

## §G · The dishonest omission

**The base-rate distribution of solo indie SaaS at cold start is bimodal, and the mode is $0 forever.** Neither report shows you the *distribution* of outcomes — only the ceiling case studies (Marc Lou $49k/mo, Danny Postma $1M exit, Theo $1M/yr). Median solo indie in month 12 has $0 MRR. Both reports pick channels by "P(best case)" and never multiply by "P(operator is median)" — which for an 18-month zero-income cold-start operator is ~70–85%. If you honestly conditioned on the operator's own track record, every single 30-day probability in D §1 should be halved.
