**Recommended default:** Rewardful. Reason: Stripe-native, no transaction tax, unlimited affiliates on the base plan, and the affiliate portal is white-label so a partner never sees you're on a $49/mo tier.

**Typical affiliate cut solo AI-dev tools offer (2026):**
- **30% recurring for 12 months** is the default that both parties expect (Jasper, HubSpot pattern) ([text.com](https://www.text.com/blog/ai-affiliate-programs/))
- **Lifetime 20–30%** is a distinctive offer that attracts serious promoters (Answrr example)
- **30% first payment only** (Vellum's model) — cheap for you, weak incentive for affiliates
- **50%** is a growth-hack tier used at launch to seed early promoters; drop to 30% after 90 days

At $9–$99 SKU, prefer **lifetime 30%** — a single active promoter compounding over 24 months is worth more to you than headline percentages.

## SECTION E — CASE STUDIES (successes + failures + base rates)

### E1. Successful solo-dev rev-share / sponsor landings (2024–2026)

1. **Danny Postma — Headlime** — solo built and sold his AI writing tool for ~$1M in 8 months without paid growth partners; used Twitter build-in-public + inbound sponsor interest ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/danny-postma-built-and-sold-ai-writing-tool-for-1m-in-8-months-full-story-7da7a7edf7)). Relevance to operator: sold-then-partnered pattern beats partner-first for solo devs.
2. **Marc Lou** — indie hacker at ~$49K/mo across 20+ services; distribution is largely Twitter build-in-public and cross-promotion with other indie hackers (rev-share via mutual affiliate deals) ([Alignify](https://alignify.co/insights/indie-hackers)).
3. **Pieter Levels (Nomad List)** — received a cold sponsor request from Matt Mullenweg (Automattic) after launching Nomad List; canonical case that traction attracts sponsors, not the other way ([Bootstrapped Founder](https://thebootstrappedfounder.com/pieter-levels-the-indie-hackers-guide-to-ai-startups/)).
4. **Arvid Kahl** — grew The Bootstrapped Founder newsletter to 7,600 subs and $3K/mo; monetized via sponsors + course sales starting well before "critical mass" ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-arvid-kahl-grew-his-newsletter-to-7-600-subscribers-3000-mo-revenue-45dab82dff)).
5. **Josh Pigford (Baremetrics → Maybe)** — after $4M Baremetrics exit, now building 5 products solo with AI agents; his current sponsors come inbound from his podcast/newsletter audience ([Creator Economy interview](https://creatoreconomy.so/p/the-exact-ai-skills-this-solo-founder-uses-josh-pigford)).
6. **Indie Hackers member case** — closed first 4-figure sponsorship on a cold pitch with 10–12K/mo pageviews ([case post](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/closed-first-4-figure-sponsorship-on-a-cold-pitch-email-ff60e2944f)). Directly relevant: cold works below "big audience" thresholds.
7. **Marketing Shots creator** — 11 cold DMs → 1 paid sponsor next day (9% conversion) ([Substack](https://marketingshots.substack.com/p/shot-159-the-cold-dm-hack-that-landed)). Confirms cold DM can outperform email for smaller creators.

### E2. Failures / negative case studies

1. **$750 newsletter sponsorship, 124 clicks, 0 sales** ([isora.me postmortem](https://www.isora.me/email-newsletter-sponsorship/)) — SaaS had ARPU $40 / LTV insufficient to break even. Lesson: run the CAC math before writing the check.
2. **Newsletter community waited "over a year" for first paying sponsor** — mentioned in Indie Hackers ["when should I start looking for sponsors"](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/when-should-i-start-looking-for-sponsors-for-my-newsletter-4f277550c9) — small-audience newsletters routinely take 6–18 months of pitching to close first sponsor.
3. **"Featured on a popular stream, 17 day-one backers"** ([Glowing Eye Games postmortem](https://www.glowingeyegames.com/great-post-mortems-for-indie-developers/)) — audience overlap ≠ purchase intent. A well-aligned creator can still under-deliver.
4. **Newsletter sponsorship variance:** creator reports show a normal distribution of outcomes from "sold out inventory" (The Neuron pre-acquisition) to "€750 for 124 clicks, 0 customers" — swing driven mostly by ICP-fit, not creator size.
5. **The Neuron itself:** the newsletter succeeded, but co-founder Pete Huang left in fall 2024 to run a medical clinic before its 2025 acquisition ([They Got Acquired](https://theygotacquired.com/content/the-neuron-acquired-by-technologyadvice/)). Reminder: even successful sponsor deals don't survive founder churn — always assume 30% chance a creator partner walks in 12 months.

### E3. Base rate: cold rev-share pitches to ~10K-follower creator

Best-available reference points from 2026 data:

- **Cold email average reply rate: 3.43%** across all industries ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)).
- **Personalized cold email:** up to **18% reply**, with top-5% senders hitting 10%+ ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)).
- **B2B cold outreach average: 27.7%** open, **~9%** generic reply, **~18%** with heavy personalization ([Martal](https://martal.ca/b2b-cold-email-statistics-lb/)).
- Cold DM (Twitter/X, LinkedIn) to niche creators: anecdotal 5–15% response for high-fit, personalized messages (Marketing Shots' 9% is representative).

**Synthesized base rate for THIS operator:** for cold rev-share pitches to newsletters/YouTubers with ~10K–100K following, expect:
- **Reply rate:** 5–12% if personalized, 1–3% if templated
- **"Interested to discuss" conversion:** ~30% of replies → 1.5–4% of pitches sent
- **"Actual deal closed":** ~20% of interested → **0.3–0.8% of pitches sent**

Practical implication: to land 3 deals in 90 days, budget **400–1,000 cold pitches** with meaningful per-pitch personalization (specific episode/issue reference, ICP match justification, price-point offered).

Operator constraints: cold-only, no personal brand, $9–99 price points, async/email preferred. What has highest P of landing in 30–90 days?

### F1 — TOP: Passionfroot creator marketplace bookings ★★★★★
- **Why highest P:** the whole platform exists to remove the "no warm network" problem. Public rate cards → book directly → no cold pitch needed for the discovery step.

- **Cash P in 30 days:** medium (revenue from booked-slot conversions; not from being paid)
- **Long-term compound:** medium — the same creators re-book once you show them your ad worked.

### F2 — Small AI newsletter sponsorships in the $200–$1,500 tier ★★★★☆
- **Why:** cold-friendly (rate cards public), affordable at operator's scale, ICP-aligned.
- **Specific first targets (in order):**
  1. **Ben's Bites tools section — $1,200** (specifically designed for indie AI tools)
  2. **Ben's Bites unclassified slot — $200** (cheapest sanity check)

  4. **CSS-Weekly AI Developer Newsletter** — small, cheap, dev-audience
  5. **daily.dev Ads** — one intake reaches many newsletters
- **Cash P in 30 days:** medium
- **Long-term compound:** high — once one sponsorship converts, use it as social proof for larger slots

### F3 — Freelance AI-engineer subcontracting (Toptal → A.Team → Arc) ★★★★☆
- **Why:** the ONLY option that gives high P of $5K–$20K in the door within 30–60 days, and it's cold-designed by construction.
- **Best-fit ordering for a solo AI-dev builder:**
  1. **Arc.dev** — apply this week; profile approval fast; you can bid on gigs within days
  2. **A.Team** — apply same week (2–4 wk approval); mission model matches "async, don't want a boss"
  3. **Toptal** — apply same week (2–3 wk vetting); highest rates once in
  4. **KORE1** — send resume; used as backup pipeline
- **Cash P in 30 days:** high (Arc is fastest)
- **Long-term compound:** medium — hourly work doesn't compound like SaaS, but it funds runway.
- **Warning:** subcontracting is a **time-for-money floor**, not a growth mechanism. Cap at 20 hrs/week or it eats the product time.

### F4 — Run YOUR OWN affiliate program on Rewardful, then recruit ★★★☆☆
- **Why:** the operator's compounding lever. Once live, every future creator conversation includes a real affiliate offer (30% lifetime) that removes their risk.
- **First move:** stand up Rewardful in an afternoon (Stripe one-click), publish a `/affiliates` landing page with the D7 template above, add the offer to the D1–D3 pitch emails.
- **Cold-friendly?** N/A — this is infrastructure that MAKES cold pitches convert better.
- **Cash P in 30 days:** low direct; multiplies P of A/B/E1
- **Long-term compound:** highest of any option in this doc. Compounding revenue from external promoters is exactly the "long-term compound" ask.

### F5 — Implementation-partner slots (Dust.tt, Baseten, Anyscale) ★★★☆☆
- **Why:** public application forms, no warm network required, decent rev share (10–20% first-year).
- **First move:** apply to all three this week; expected reply within 2–4 wk.
- **Cash P in 30 days:** low (sales cycle is long)
- **Cash P in 90 days:** medium (first referral fees possible)
- **Long-term compound:** medium — being listed on a public partner directory becomes a passive lead source.
