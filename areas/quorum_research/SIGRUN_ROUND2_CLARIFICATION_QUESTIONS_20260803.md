## My L2 call (Q3-C authority)

⭐ **FOCUS-ONE, not shotgun. One product until it has a repeatable cold
acquisition channel — then clone the channel, not the product.**

**Reasoning, from the library rather than taste:** John Rush's 24 directories look
like a shotgun, but they share **one** distribution engine — an SEO/directory
network that amortizes across every product. Levels, Lou, Postma and Dinh all
amortize across an **audience**. ⭐ **You have neither. Cold-only means each new
product is its own distribution problem, so N products = N distribution problems,
not one.** Against a **54%-at-zero** Stripe-verified base rate, a shotgun just
buys N draws from a losing distribution.

**My only true cold templates are AppAlchemy** ($17k MRR, one product, Reddit,
$0 paid, no pre-audience) **and Habit Pixel** ($1k MRR in 8 months). Both are
focus-one.

**Product shape:** something whose acquisition is **search/directory-shaped**, not
sales-shaped — because search compounds without an audience and is the one channel
that works cold. ⛔ **Not a Papermark or Documenso fork** — both are AGPL-3.0 with
a network-source obligation (row 94), and both need a sales motion you don't have
yet. **Veto this and I'll re-plan; it's your studio.**
