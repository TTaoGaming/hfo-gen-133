## What it does NOT add — and never will

`SEND` · `SPEND` · `PUBLISH` · `PUSH` · `SEAL` · `IMMUNIZE` · `DELETE`

**Invariant CAN-2 holds without exception: the irreversible set never shrinks.**
Autonomy grows *inside* the reversible envelope, never by expanding it. A raised
ceiling is more reach, not more finality.

This matters more here than anywhere else in the fleet, because
`substrate_coordinator` is the widest reach any agent has been given. Piloting a
GUI means Olrún can *physically reach* buttons that would send, spend, or
publish. The ceiling is what stands between reach and effect, and it is the only
thing standing there — a GUI has no gate.

## Invariant OL-7 — the new one

> **Reach is not authority.** Olrún can click a Send button; she may not. When a
> workflow's next step is an irreversible effect, she **stops and pages the
> operator**, with the UI parked at that step. Preparing is not doing.
